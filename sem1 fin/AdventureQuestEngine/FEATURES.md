## Getting Started

### Character Creation

#### Warrior
**Playstyle**: Melee tank with high HP
- Starting HP: 150
- Starting Attack: 15
- Starting Defense: 8
- **Best for**: Beginners, tanking, sustained damage

**Specializations**:
- **Berserker**: High risk, high reward damage dealer
- **Tank**: Defensive powerhouse with shields
- **Bladestorm**: AoE damage and mobility

#### Mage
**Playstyle**: Ranged magic damage dealer
- Starting HP: 80
- Starting Magic Power: 20
- Starting Mana: 100
- **Best for**: Burst damage, elemental combos

**Specializations**:
- **Pyromancer**: Fire damage and burning DoTs
- **Cryomancer**: Ice control and crowd control
- **Arcanist**: Pure magical power and mana efficiency

#### Rogue
**Playstyle**: High crit, high dodge assassin
- Starting HP: 100
- Starting Crit Chance: 25%
- Starting Dodge: 20%
- **Best for**: Critical hits, avoiding damage

**Specializations**:
- **Assassin**: Single-target burst damage
- **Shadowdancer**: Evasion and stealth mechanics
- **Poisoner**: Damage-over-time and debuffs

#### Paladin
**Playstyle**: Balanced with healing abilities
- Starting HP: 120
- Starting Attack: 12
- Starting Defense: 7
- **Best for**: Solo play, sustain, versatility

**Specializations**:
- **Crusader**: Holy damage against undead
- **Protector**: Healing and team support
- **Justicar**: Balanced offense and defense

## Combat System

### Combat Positions

#### Front Line
- **Damage**: +10% melee damage
- **Defense**: Takes full enemy damage
- **Best for**: Warriors, Paladins

#### Mid Line
- **Damage**: Normal damage
- **Defense**: 90% enemy damage
- **Best for**: Balanced classes

#### Back Line
- **Damage**: +15% ranged/magic damage
- **Defense**: 75% enemy damage
- **Restriction**: Can only use ranged/magic attacks
- **Best for**: Mages, Archers

### Combat Actions

#### Basic Attack
- Standard damage
- Builds combo meter
- No resource cost

#### Heavy Attack
- +50% damage
- -20% accuracy
- Great for finishing enemies

#### Quick Attack
- -30% damage
- +20% accuracy
- +10% dodge chance next turn
- Good for building combos safely

#### Defend
- +50% defense
- +30% dodge chance
- Reduces damage taken
- Use when low on HP

#### Use Skill
- Consumes mana
- Powerful effects
- Has cooldowns
- Can change battle flow

### Elemental System

#### Damage Elements

**Physical** → No special effects, reliable
**Fire** → Burns for 3 turns (10% max HP per turn)
**Ice** → Slows enemy (-20% speed), can freeze
**Lightning** → Chains to nearby enemies (50% damage)
**Poison** → Strong DoT (15% max HP per turn for 5 turns)
**Holy** → +50% damage vs undead
**Shadow** → Lifesteal (25% of damage healed)
**Arcane** → Pure magic, ignores some resistance

#### Elemental Combos

- **Wet (Water) + Lightning** → 2x lightning damage + chain
- **Burning + Wind** → Spread fire to all enemies
- **Ice + Physical** → Shatter for +50% damage
- **Poison + Fire** → Explosion for burst damage
- **Holy + Shadow** → Massive damage but hurts caster

### Combo System

Build your combo meter (0-100):
- Basic attack: +10 combo
- Critical hit: +15 combo
- Skill use: +20 combo
- Hit without taking damage: +5 combo

**Combo Thresholds**:
- 25 combo: +10% damage
- 50 combo: +25% damage
- 75 combo: +50% damage
- 100 combo: **FINISHER AVAILABLE!**

**Combo Finishers** (costs 100 combo):
- Warrior: Devastating Blow (300% damage)
- Mage: Arcane Explosion (200% AoE damage)
- Rogue: Assassinate (500% damage + guaranteed crit)
- Paladin: Divine Judgment (250% damage + full heal)

### Enemy Intents

Enemies telegraph their actions:
- 🗡️ **Light Attack**: Minor damage incoming
- ⚔️ **Heavy Attack**: Major damage, consider defending
- 🛡️ **Defend**: Enemy reducing damage taken
- 💀 **Debuff**: Status effect incoming
- 💪 **Buff**: Enemy strengthening itself
- ❤️ **Heal**: Enemy restoring HP
- 🎯 **Control**: Stun or disabling ability
- 👥 **Summon**: Enemy calling reinforcements
- ⚡ **Charge**: Building up for ultimate attack

## Skill Trees & Talents

### How Talents Work

- Earn 1 talent point per level
- Each talent has 1-5 ranks
- Prerequisites unlock higher tiers
- Can respec anytime (costs gold)

### Talent Types

#### Passive Talents
- Always active
- Stack multiplicatively
- Examples: +5% HP per rank, +2% crit per rank

#### Active Abilities
- Unlocks usable skills
- Have cooldowns
- Consume mana
- Examples: Whirlwind, Fireball, Backstab

#### Ultimate Talents
- Tier 7 capstones
- Extremely powerful
- Long cooldowns
- Define your playstyle

### Example Talent Paths

#### Warrior - Berserker Path
1. **Bloodlust** (Passive): +5% damage per rank
2. **Reckless Charge** (Active): Gap closer, 150% damage
3. **Enrage** (Active): +50% damage, -25% defense for 5 turns
4. **Execute** (Active): Instant kill under 20% HP
5. **Rampage** (Ultimate): Attack all enemies, +200% damage

#### Mage - Pyromancer Path
1. **Fire Mastery** (Passive): +10% fire damage per rank
2. **Fireball** (Active): 180% damage + burn
3. **Flame Ward** (Active): +50% fire resist, reflect 25% damage
4. **Combustion** (Active): Detonate all burns for massive damage
5. **Meteor Storm** (Ultimate): AoE 400% fire damage

## Crafting & Enchanting

### Crafting Basics

#### Recipe Discovery
- Find recipes in treasure chests
- Learn from books
- Buy from merchants
- Unlock through quests
- Some recipes are hidden!

#### Material Gathering
- Defeat enemies for materials
- Gather in specific biomes
- Disenchant unwanted items
- Trade with merchants

#### Crafting Stations
- **Forge**: Weapons and armor
- **Alchemy Lab**: Potions and elixirs
- **Enchanting Table**: Add enchantments
- **Workbench**: Accessories and tools

### Enchanting System

#### How to Enchant
1. Have an item (not already max enchanted)
2. Gather enchanting materials
3. Visit enchanting table
4. Choose enchantment type
5. Pay gold cost
6. Success chance based on level

#### Popular Enchantments

**For Weapons**:
- **Sharpness**: +10% damage per level
- **Fire Damage**: +15 fire damage per level
- **Lifesteal**: Heal 5% of damage dealt per level
- **Critical Strike**: +3% crit chance per level

**For Armor**:
- **Protection**: +5% damage reduction per level
- **Thorns**: Reflect 10% damage per level
- **Regeneration**: +2 HP per turn per level
- **Unbreaking**: Item never degrades

**For Accessories**:
- **Luck**: +10% better loot per level
- **Experience**: +10% XP gain per level
- **Speed**: +5 speed per level
- **Mana Recovery**: +5 mana per turn per level

### Upgrading Items

Enhance items from +0 to +10:
- Each +1 adds 10% stats
- Costs increase exponentially
- Requires special materials
- +10 items glow with power!

## Companions

### Recruiting Companions

Find companions in:
- Taverns in safe rooms
- After completing quests
- Rescuing them from enemies
- Faction rewards

### Companion Loyalty

**Loyalty Levels** (0-100):
- **0-20**: Angry (-20% effectiveness, may leave)
- **21-40**: Unhappy (-10% effectiveness)
- **41-60**: Neutral (normal)
- **61-80**: Happy (+10% effectiveness)
- **81-100**: Devoted (+25% effectiveness, special ability unlocked)

**Increasing Loyalty**:
- Make choices they approve of
- Complete their personal quests
- Give them gifts
- Fight alongside them
- Talk to them regularly

**Decreasing Loyalty**:
- Make choices they disapprove of
- Let them die in combat
- Ignore their requests
- Betray their values

### Companion Types

#### Archer Companion (Lyra Swiftarrow)
- Ranged DPS
- **Ability**: Volley (hits all enemies for 60% damage)
- **Passive**: +10% player dodge chance
- **Likes**: Helping innocents, precision
- **Dislikes**: Recklessness, cruelty

#### Healer Companion (Brother Aldric)
- Support healer
- **Ability**: Mass Heal (restore 40 HP to player)
- **Passive**: +15% healing received
- **Likes**: Mercy, peace
- **Dislikes**: Evil choices, killing unnecessarily

#### Tank Companion (Gorath Ironshield)
- Defensive wall
- **Ability**: Shield Wall (blocks next attack)
- **Passive**: +10% player defense
- **Likes**: Honor, bravery
- **Dislikes**: Cowardice, theft

### Companion Combat

- Companions act after you each turn
- They use abilities intelligently
- Can't be directly controlled
- Revive if you finish battle
- Gain XP and level up with you

## Factions

### Major Factions

#### Knights Order (Lawful Good)
- **Ideology**: Honor, justice, protection
- **Rewards**: Holy weapons, heavy armor
- **Conflicts with**: Thieves Guild, Cult of Shadows
- **Unique Ability**: Divine Shield

#### Thieves Guild (Chaotic Neutral)
- **Ideology**: Freedom, cunning, profit
- **Rewards**: Stealth gear, rare items
- **Conflicts with**: Knights Order, Merchants League
- **Unique Ability**: Shadow Step

#### Mages Circle (Neutral)
- **Ideology**: Knowledge, power, research
- **Rewards**: Spellbooks, enchanted items
- **Conflicts with**: None (stays neutral)
- **Unique Ability**: Arcane Insight

#### Cult of Shadows (Evil)
- **Ideology**: Power at any cost, domination
- **Rewards**: Dark artifacts, powerful curses
- **Conflicts with**: Knights Order, Dragon Riders
- **Unique Ability**: Drain Life

### Reputation Ranks

**Hostile (-3)**: Attack on sight, cannot trade
**Unfriendly (-2)**: Refuse service, higher prices
**Neutral (0)**: Standard service, normal prices
**Friendly (1)**: -10% prices, basic quests
**Honored (2)**: -20% prices, good quests, special items
**Revered (3)**: -30% prices, rare quests, exclusive items
**Exalted (4)**: -40% prices, legendary quests, faction ability

### Gaining Reputation

- Complete faction quests (+50-500 rep)
- Donate gold (+1 rep per 10 gold)
- Kill faction enemies (+10-50 rep)
- Wear faction tabard (+reputation gain bonus)
- Make choices aligned with faction values

## Biomes & Environment

### Biome Types & Features

#### Dungeon Depths (Starting Area)
- **Enemies**: Goblins, Rats, Slimes
- **Hazards**: Minor traps
- **Materials**: Basic ores, common herbs
- **Best for**: Learning the game

#### Volcanic Caverns
- **Enemies**: Fire elementals, Lava golems
- **Hazards**: Lava pools (10 damage per turn)
- **Materials**: Volcanic glass, Fire essence
- **Bonus**: +50% fire damage, -50% ice damage
- **Best for**: Pyromancers

#### Icy Halls
- **Enemies**: Ice wraiths, Frozen undead
- **Hazards**: Ice patches (chance to slip and skip turn)
- **Materials**: Ice crystals, Frost herbs
- **Bonus**: +50% ice damage, slowed movement
- **Best for**: Cryomancers

#### Crystal Mines
- **Enemies**: Crystal spiders, Gem golems
- **Hazards**: Cave-ins
- **Materials**: Gems, Rare crystals
- **Bonus**: +25% gold drops
- **Best for**: Resource gathering

### Weather Effects

#### Fog
- -20% accuracy for everyone
- Reduced vision
- Higher ambush chance

#### Storm
- Random lightning strikes (20 damage)
- +50% lightning damage
- Water effects empowered

#### Blood Moon
- Enemies +30% stronger
- +100% rare drop chance
- Undead enemies more common

## Challenge Modes

### Ironman Mode
- **Rules**: One life, permadeath, no saving
- **Rewards**: 2x achievement points, "Ironborn" title
- **Tips**: Play very cautiously, use defensive builds

### Speedrun Mode
- **Rules**: Complete in under 500 turns
- **Rewards**: Leaderboard entry, "Speed Demon" title
- **Tips**: Skip optional content, optimize routes, use Rogue class

### Pacifist Mode
- **Rules**: Win with under 10 total kills
- **Rewards**: "Pacifist" title, unique dialogue options
- **Tips**: Use stealth, diplomacy, complete quests instead of combat

### Glass Cannon Mode
- **Rules**: Deal and receive 2x damage
- **Rewards**: 1.5x gold and XP, "Glass Cannon" title
- **Tips**: Kill enemies before they hit you, use ranged classes

## Tips & Strategies

### Early Game (Levels 1-10)
1. **Focus on exploration**: Find all safe rooms
2. **Complete easy quests**: Build gold and XP
3. **Don't spend gold yet**: Save for better items later
4. **Unlock talents evenly**: Don't over-specialize too early
5. **Fight everything**: You need the XP

### Mid Game (Levels 11-25)
1. **Choose specialization**: Commit to a build
2. **Start crafting**: Make your own gear
3. **Recruit companion**: They make a huge difference
4. **Join a faction**: Pick one aligned with your playstyle
5. **Enchant your best gear**: Focus on 2-3 key items

### Late Game (Levels 26+)
1. **Max out talent tree**: Complete your spec
2. **Get legendary items**: Farm or craft them
3. **Max faction reputation**: Unlock all faction rewards
4. **Complete achievements**: Unlock permanent perks
5. **Prepare for boss**: Stock up on consumables

### Combat Tips
- **Save mana for important fights**: Don't waste it on trash mobs
- **Position matters**: Back line for mages, front for warriors
- **Watch enemy intent**: Defend when heavy attack incoming
- **Build combos**: Don't waste finishers on weak enemies
- **Use environment**: Some biomes favor certain strategies
- **Companion synergy**: Pair complementary companions

### Gold Management
- **Don't buy common items**: Craft them instead
- **Invest in crafting materials**: They appreciate in value
- **Reputation discounts**: Get Honored before shopping
- **Sell wisely**: Keep rare items for crafting/disenchanting
- **Quest rewards**: Often better than buying

### Optimal Builds

#### Warrior DPS
- Specialization: Berserker
- Stats: Attack > Crit > HP
- Talents: Bloodlust, Enrage, Execute
- Gear: High damage weapons, crit accessories
- Companion: Healer

#### Mage Burst
- Specialization: Pyromancer or Arcanist
- Stats: Magic Power > Mana > Crit
- Talents: Elemental mastery, Arcane power
- Gear: Magic weapons, mana regen
- Companion: Tank

#### Rogue Assassin
- Specialization: Assassin or Shadowdancer
- Stats: Crit > Attack > Dodge
- Talents: Backstab, Shadow strike, Assassinate
- Gear: Fast weapons, crit gear
- Companion: Tank or Archer
