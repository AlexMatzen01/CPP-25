# CHANGELOG - Adventure Quest Engine

## 🎮 Version 2.0 - "MASSIVE OVERHAUL" (December 2025)

### 🌟 MAJOR NEW FEATURES

#### Combat System Revolution
- ✨ **Elemental Damage System**
  - 8 damage types: Physical, Fire, Ice, Lightning, Poison, Holy, Shadow, Arcane
  - Elemental resistances and vulnerabilities
  - Elemental reactions and combos
  - Biome-specific elemental advantages

- ⚔️ **Advanced Combat Mechanics**
  - Combo attack chains with multipliers
  - Tactical positioning (Front/Mid/Back line)
  - Heavy attacks, quick strikes, defensive stance
  - Counterattack system on successful dodges
  - Multi-target and AoE abilities
  - Enhanced enemy AI with telegraphed intents
  - Critical hit animations and effects

#### Skill Tree & Progression
- 🌳 **Class Specialization Trees**
  - Warrior: Berserker / Tank / Bladestorm
  - Mage: Pyromancer / Cryomancer / Arcanist
  - Rogue: Assassin / Shadowdancer / Poisoner
  - Paladin: Crusader / Protector / Justicar

- 💎 **70+ Unique Talents**
  - Active abilities with cooldowns
  - Passive stat bonuses
  - Ultimate capstone abilities
  - Synergy bonuses between talents
  - Unlimited respeccing

#### Crafting & Economy
- 🔨 **Comprehensive Crafting System**
  - 100+ craftable items
  - Recipe discovery and learning
  - Material gathering from biomes
  - Crafting stations (Forge, Alchemy Lab, etc.)
  - Success/failure mechanics

- ✨ **Enchanting System**
  - 15+ enchantment types
  - Up to 3 enchantments per item
  - Item upgrading (+0 to +10)
  - Enchantment levels (1-5)
  - Item durability and repair

- 💰 **Dynamic Economy**
  - Supply and demand pricing
  - Bartering and trading
  - Reputation discounts
  - Black market for rare items
  - Traveling merchants with unique stock

#### Companions & Factions
- 👥 **Companion System**
  - 10+ recruitable companions
  - 6 companion classes (Archer, Healer, Tank, Mage, Thief, Beast)
  - Loyalty and mood system
  - Personal companion quests
  - Combat assistance
  - Companion bonuses (XP, Gold, Loot)

- 🏰 **Faction Reputation**
  - 7 major factions
  - Reputation ranks (Hostile to Exalted)
  - Faction-specific quests and rewards
  - Faction conflicts and allegiances
  - Unlockable titles and tabards

#### Dynamic World
- 🌍 **Biome System**
  - 10 unique biomes with distinct mechanics
  - Environmental hazards (lava, poison gas, ice patches)
  - Biome-specific enemies and loot
  - Resource gathering per biome
  - Environmental combat modifiers

- 🌦️ **Weather System**
  - 9 weather types affecting gameplay
  - Combat stat modifications
  - Visibility and accuracy changes
  - Random weather events
  - Blood moons and auroras

- 🗿 **Interactive Landmarks**
  - Shrines (healing and buffs)
